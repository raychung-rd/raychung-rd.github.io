abstract: 'We developed an LLM-powered chatbot using a human-centered design approach to support older adults in a retirement community with low tech and eHealth literacy. The chatbot offers accessible, conversational assistance for both navigating community resources and improving digital literacy. Features like large text, high-contrast themes, and voice interaction enhance usability. In a pilot trial, residents successfully completed tasks and reported high satisfaction, suggesting the chatbot's potential to empower older adults through intuitive, low-barrier technology.'
