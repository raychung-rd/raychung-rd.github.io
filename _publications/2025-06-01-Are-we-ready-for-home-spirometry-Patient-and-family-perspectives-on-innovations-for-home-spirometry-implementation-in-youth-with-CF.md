abstract: 'Participants rated standardized implementation of pediatric home spirometry as highly feasible and acceptable, valuing its accessibility for self-monitoring. However, many reported challenges incorporating it into daily routines. Enthusiastic, timely support from interdisciplinary care teams was a key program facilitator, underscoring the need for dedicated staff to sustain family engagement and ensure successful integration into routine care. These findings highlight lived experience with home spirometry and suggest ways to strengthen implementation in the future.'
